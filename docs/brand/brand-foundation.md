# MPReply brand foundation and identity brief

## Brand idea

A calm operating system for marketplace communication: fast enough for automation, controlled enough for customer-facing work, transparent enough to trust.

Attributes: precise, calm, competent, operational, accountable, modern without trend-chasing. Not a playful AI toy, neon cyberpunk, generic purple/cyan SaaS, beige editorial template or marketplace clone.

## Voice

Concrete verbs, clear outcomes, explicit limitations, native Russian and evidence before adjectives. Avoid “революционный”, “магический”, “безошибочный”, unsupported guarantees, excessive exclamation marks and generated filler.

## Required visual research

Before final identity: produce at least three distinct directions. Each needs strategic idea, physical use scene, typography direction, color strategy, logo principle, icon/illustration language, product implications, marketing implications and anti-reference explanation. Do not approve logo/palette/font from one attractive mockup.

## Logo brief

Current rounded `R` is placeholder. Final package needs wordmark, compact symbol if justified, monochrome/light/dark variants, clearspace, minimum-size guidance, editable SVG and no copied competitor/marketplace geometry. Explore response, return, signal, controlled flow, acknowledgement or routing. Avoid generic chat bubble/sparkle AI clichés.

## Color

Choose strategy before colors: restrained, committed, full palette or drenched. Product defaults toward restraint; marketing may be more expressive. Tokens mandatory, status colors distinct from brand, charts not hue-only, no generic purple/cyan AI gradient and no sand/cream merely to signal premium.

## Typography

Excellent Cyrillic, dense UI legibility, useful numerals, strong display voice without harming product readability. Self-host only with license verified. Render Russian marketing copy, tables, forms and legal text before approval.

## Iconography and imagery

One coherent icon family. Prefer real product UI, useful diagrams and restrained abstract compositions tied to response/routing. Avoid generic 3D blobs, fake device mockups, stock support teams and hand-drawn SVG fallback art.

## Motion

Communicate state, continuity and causality. Avoid scroll hijacking, bounce, mandatory smooth-scroll libraries and layout-property animation by reflex. Reduced motion mandatory.

## Approval gates

Three directions → one approved direction → favicon-size geometry review → Cyrillic typography/contrast review → marketing/dashboard applications → asset export matrix → basic trademark screening → editable sources preserved.
