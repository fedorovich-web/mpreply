# Legal readiness notes

These notes are not legal advice.

Before production launch, verify at minimum:

1. Identity and details of the actual operator/service provider.
2. Applicable personal-data obligations, including the current version of 152-FZ and related rules.
3. Whether notification/registration duties apply to the operator.
4. Actual data flows, processors, sub-processors, LLM provider routing and marketplace API terms.
5. Storage location and localization requirements based on the real infrastructure.
6. Retention periods and deletion/export workflows for every data category.
7. Consent and notice requirements for public forms, cookies, analytics and marketing.
8. DPA/processor terms for customer organizations where required.
9. Security incident and data-breach response obligations.
10. Final Terms, Privacy Policy, tariff/payment terms, SLA/support and liability model.

The public legal pages in Sprint 8 intentionally avoid inventing company details, storage facts, guarantees or final legal positions that have not yet been confirmed.
